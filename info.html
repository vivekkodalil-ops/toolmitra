About ToolMitra

ToolMitra is a free online platform that provides simple calculators and useful tools for everyday needs.

Our goal is to make common calculations easier, faster and easier to understand without requiring users to create an account.

What ToolMitra provides

  • Financial and investment calculators
  • Loan and EMI related calculators
  • Tax and GST related calculators
  • Health and personal calculators
  • Vehicle and fuel calculators
  • Everyday calculation tools
  • Business and sales calculators

Our approach

ToolMitra is designed with simplicity and usability in mind. Our tools are intended to provide quick, understandable results for common calculations.

Many calculations can be performed directly in your browser without requiring an account or registration.

Calculator results are intended for general informational purposes and should not be considered professional financial, tax, medical or legal advice.
